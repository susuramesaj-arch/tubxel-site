# TUBXEL Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

TUBXEL Yonetici is a private tool used only by the TUBXEL owner to manage the owner's own YouTube channels.

What we access: with the owner's permission, the tool uses the YouTube API to upload videos, edit titles, descriptions and thumbnails, and read the statistics of the owner's own channels.

What we store: only encrypted access tokens belonging to the owner, kept in a private storage. We do not collect, sell, share or publish any personal data of other people or visitors.

Third parties: the tool uses Google and YouTube services. Use of YouTube data follows the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access: the owner can revoke access at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions
