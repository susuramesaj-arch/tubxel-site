# TUBXEL

TUBXEL is an independent media brand that publishes short and long educational videos on YouTube and Instagram.

This site is the home page of the TUBXEL Yonetici tool, a private tool used only by the TUBXEL owner to manage the TUBXEL YouTube channels (upload videos, edit titles and descriptions, read channel statistics). It is not offered to the public.
